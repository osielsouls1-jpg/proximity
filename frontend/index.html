Safety

Location is power. We take that seriously.

One tap to disappear from the map. Existing matches won't see you until you toggle back.

FAQ

Questions you'd ask a friend.

Isn't sharing live location kind of dangerous? +
Live location only shows after both people mutually match. You can go invisible at any time. You can block instantly. Location precision is also throttled — matches see you within ~10–50 feet, not your exact GPS coordinates.
When does it launch? +
Public launch is targeted for summer 2026. Waitlist members get access 2 weeks early in their city, starting with NYC, LA, Austin, Miami, and SF.
Is it free? +
Yes, free to use. A Plus tier ($9.99/mo) unlocks unlimited likes, advanced filters, and a wider radius. We don't sell your data — ever.
What if I just want to play games and not date? +
Set your profile to "friends only" mode and Proximity becomes a Pokémon GO for finding people to play games with locally. No pressure, no expectations.
How do you make money? +
The Plus subscription, and partnerships with local venues (think: "match here and get a free drink"). No ads in profiles. No selling data.
